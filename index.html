PRIVACY POLICY TERMS OF USE CONTACT

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the game Indian Scary Teacher (“the Game”), made and published by GameDev Bhai (“we”, “us”), handles your information.

1. What we collect

We collect as little as possible. We do not ask for your email, phone number, contacts, location or an account, and the Game itself has no analytics (the ads in section 6 are the only exception: Google collects what it needs to show them). The only information that leaves your device is what the online leaderboard (section 3) and multiplayer (section 4) need, and what Google AdMob uses to show ads (section 6). If a future update collects anything more, this policy will be updated first and the new version will be shown inside the Game.

2. What stays on your device

Your acceptance of these terms and your settings are saved only on your own device, inside the Game’s private storage. Your profile picture and your playthroughs are saved on your device too and, while you take part in the online leaderboard, shared in your player profile (section 3). You can delete all of it at any time by clearing the Game’s data in your phone’s settings or by uninstalling the Game.

3. Online leaderboard

The leaderboard shows the fastest escapes of players around the world. Every mode – Easy, Normal, Hard and Practice – has its own boards: All time, Weekly (resets every Monday, 00:00 UTC) and Daily (resets every day, 00:00 UTC). Your in-game profile shows your world rank in every mode. Every day brings 3 new daily challenges (the same for every player, new at 00:00 UTC). Players who complete all 3 appear on that day’s Challenge board with their player name, picture and the time they finished, in the order they finished (it has no ranks and resets every day). Your achievements are saved on your device; while ONLINE is on, the number of achievements you have unlocked (for example 12 / 41) is shown in your player profile. Your tier (Garbage, Plastic, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond or Legendary) is worked out from your All time rank and is shown to other players together with your rank, also when ONLINE is off (it comes from the public leaderboard).

  • What is sent: when you escape, the Game sends your player name, your escape time and the mode (Easy, Normal, Hard or Practice), together with an anonymous player ID that Unity creates for your installation. Your best time on each board is kept. Other players can see your player name, time and date on the boards of that mode.
  • Your player profile: other players can tap your name on the leaderboard to see your player profile: your profile picture (a small copy), your player name, your world rank in every mode and your successful playthroughs (time, mode and date of your newest 100 escapes). Your picture is also shown next to your name on the leaderboard.
  • Who stores it: the leaderboard and the player profiles run on Unity Gaming Services (Leaderboards and Cloud Save) by Unity Technologies, which stores this data on our behalf as a processor and, like any online service, receives your device’s IP address when the Game connects. See Unity’s privacy policy.
  • Retention: Weekly and Daily entries are removed from those boards when they reset. All time entries are kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • Your choice: you can switch ONLINE: OFF in your in-game profile at any time; new escapes then stay only on your device, your playthroughs become private and your picture is removed from your online profile (only times already on the leaderboard stay visible). Escapes made without internet are sent later (a Weekly or Daily time only while that week or day is still running).
  • Deleting your online data: send us your Online ID (shown in your in-game profile – tap it to copy) using the contact options below, and we will delete your leaderboard entries, your shared playthroughs and your shared picture.
  • What counts: an escape on Easy, Normal or Hard counts for that mode’s boards only with the teacher and the kid both at home; every Practice escape counts for the Practice boards. Escapes that use a test ending or a debug tool never count.
  • Fair play: please choose a player name and a profile picture that are not offensive, do not pretend to be someone else and do not show other people without their permission, and do not post times with cheats or modified versions. We may remove names, pictures or times that break these rules.

4. Multiplayer (play with a friend)

MULTIPLAYER lets two friends play one match together on two phones (one plays the student, the other plays the teacher). There is no chat and no voice.

  • Friend code: when you open MULTIPLAYER, the Game makes a short friend code for you and saves it with Unity Cloud Save together with your player name and the time you were last on the menu (friends see this as ONLINE or OFFLINE). Anyone who types your code sees your player name, profile picture and tier. Resetting the Game’s data gives you a new code.
  • Online players and friends: while you are on the menu or in a match (and ONLINE is on in your profile), the Game saves with Unity Cloud Save your player name, your tier and best world rank, when you were last online and what you are doing (on the menu, waiting to play, in a match). Other players see this in FRIENDS > ONLINE NOW with your profile picture. Turn off SHOWN IN ONLINE PLAYERS (in FRIENDS) and only your friends see you there.
  • Friend requests and friend list: a request you send saves the other player’s anonymous player ID (up to 3 at a time, for up to 7 days). Your friend list (anonymous player IDs, up to 50) and the requests you declined or friends you removed are saved too, so the other player’s phone can see the answer. When you open your room for friends, its room code is saved so your friends can join; it is removed when the room closes.
  • Invites: an invite you send saves your friend’s anonymous player ID and a room code; it is valid for one minute.
  • During a match: the two phones exchange positions and game events (doors, windows, the result) through Unity Relay. Nothing about a match is recorded or stored, and a match never counts for the leaderboard, achievements or daily challenges.
  • Your screen, live (the student only): while the teacher player watches through the student’s eyes (at the start of a match, before the teacher player takes over, and during the getaway), the student’s phone sends pictures of its game screen (up to 480 lines tall, like a 480p video, up to 15 a second) to the friend’s phone through Unity Relay. They are shown once and never saved or recorded, by the Game or by us. Nothing outside the Game is ever captured. This uses extra mobile data on both phones while it runs.
  • Getting back into a match: if the app closes during a match, your phone keeps a small note on the device (the match, your friend’s player ID and name, where you were and how far your puzzles got) for a few minutes, so opening the Game again puts you straight back in. It is deleted when the match ends.
  • Who stores it: Unity Gaming Services (Cloud Save and Relay) by Unity Technologies, as for the leaderboard (section 3).
  • Deleting it: send us your Online ID (section 3) and we delete your friend code, online status and friend list together with your leaderboard data.

5. Profile picture and photos

Adding a profile picture is optional. Only when you tap the picture circle does the Game open your device’s own photo picker, and it only reads the single picture you select. The Game makes a small square copy of it on your device for your profile and does not access, scan or upload any other photos or files. While you take part in the online leaderboard (ONLINE: ON), a small copy of that one picture is shared so other players can see it on the leaderboard and in your player profile; with ONLINE: OFF it is removed online and stays only on your device. Please do not use a picture that shows personal details such as your address, school or phone number.

6. Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC) to pay for its development. There are never any ads in multiplayer matches, and PRO players (section 7) never see an ad at all. In single player:

  • Ads you choose (rewarded): a short video you can watch for a reward – REVIVE after you are caught, LOCATE for 30 seconds, a water gun or pepper spray refill, one CONTINUE in Flappy Teacher, and MULTIPLAYER, which one ad unlocks for 24 hours on your phone (after that it asks for an ad again). Watching is always your choice; if you close the ad early, you do not get the reward.
  • Full-screen ads: one when a night starts (after the story) and, while you play, about one every 5 minutes – only at a calm moment (never during a chase, while you hide or while a panel is open), always with a short “AD BREAK” countdown first. The game is paused while an ad is on screen.
  • What Google collects: to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language, approximate location (from the IP address) and how you interact with ads. We do not receive this information. Google’s handling of it is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Your consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Google’s consent form asks you before your data may be used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time with the Ad privacy link in PLAY > Game Setup (shown where it applies).
  • Your device settings: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, at any time in your device’s settings (on most Android phones: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Suitable ads: the Game asks Google to show only ads suitable for teens (rated T or lower).

Ads need an internet connection; without one the Game can still be played.

7. PRO (in-app purchase) and redeem codes

PRO is an optional one-time purchase (the price is shown in the Game and in Google Play, for example ₹99). It removes every ad and unlocks multiplayer forever, unlimited revive, free water gun and pepper spray refills, free LOCATE and Flappy Teacher CONTINUE, and the PRO ring round your profile picture. There is no subscription.

  • Payment: handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. Google Play’s refund policies apply.
  • Your purchase: it belongs to your Google account and is restored automatically when you reinstall the Game with the same account.
  • Purchase check: when you buy PRO, the Game sends Google Play’s purchase receipt to our server on Unity Cloud Code (Unity Gaming Services). It checks Google’s signature and saves a coded copy of the purchase token together with your anonymous player ID, so that one purchase cannot be shared by many players. We never receive your card or payment details.
  • The PRO ring: when you are PRO, a “PRO” mark is added to your shared profile data, so other players see the ring round your picture (leaderboard, profiles, friends, multiplayer).
  • Redeem codes: we may give 8-digit codes (for example to content creators) that unlock PRO for free. Each code works once: when you redeem it, the code and a short anonymous tag of your device are saved with your public player data on Unity Cloud Save, so that nobody else can use it again. Codes have no cash value, may not be sold or traded, and codes that are shared publicly or misused may be cancelled.

8. Internet and links

The Game can be played offline; the online leaderboard, multiplayer and ads need an internet connection. Buttons such as YouTube, Discord and Instagram open those websites or apps outside the Game. Those services are run by other companies and have their own terms and privacy policies.

9. Permissions

The Game does not ask for permission to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, phone calls or SMS. On some devices the system photo picker is shown when you choose a profile picture; it is only used for that one picture. On Android 13 and newer, the Game asks for permission to show notifications only if you say yes to the daily reminder. The reminder is made on your phone (at most one a day, from your own challenges, streak and ranks); nothing is sent anywhere for it, and you can switch it off on the DAILY CHALLENGES screen or in your phone’s settings. For the ads, the Game uses internet access and, on newer Android versions, the advertising ID permission (added by Google’s ads software). For PRO, the Game uses Google Play’s billing permission.

10. Children

You must be at least 13 years old to use the online leaderboard and multiplayer. If you are under 13, keep ONLINE: OFF in your in-game profile and do not use MULTIPLAYER; the rest of the Game works the same. Apart from the player name and times on the online leaderboard, the Game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The Game contains horror themes (jump scares, a chasing character, a dark house) and may not be suitable for young children; parents and guardians should decide whether it is right for their child. The Game is not made for children under 13, and its ads are limited to content suitable for teens (section 6).

11. Your choices and deleting your data

Data on your device: clear the Game’s data or uninstall the Game to delete it. Data on the online leaderboard: switch ONLINE: OFF to stop sending new times and to make your playthroughs private, and send us your Online ID to have your entries, shared playthroughs and shared picture deleted. You can contact us with any privacy question at indianscaryteacher@gmail.com or using the other details below.
